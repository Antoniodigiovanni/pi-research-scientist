# Project research rules

Read `research.yaml` before using data or external services. This file sets project-specific policy and complements the installed global research rules.

Keep the current research question in `docs/research-question.md`, important choices in `docs/decisions/`, dataset knowledge in `docs/data/`, literature evidence in `literature/`, experiment evidence in `experiments/`, and publication claims in `paper/claims.md`. Link these records rather than relying on conversation history.

Do not weaken `internal` data controls or change the project to `public` without a documented owner decision. Do not commit secrets, raw restricted data, generated credentials, or local environment files.
