# Venue adaptation

Retrieve current official author instructions and the official template from the venue or publisher. Use `pi-web-access` for retrieval and source checking. Record the access date and authoritative URLs in `paper/venue.md`; venue rules change.

Extract and verify:

- scope and contribution fit;
- page or word limits and what counts toward them;
- anonymity and submission identity rules;
- required sections, statements, checklists, and forms;
- supplement, artifact, data, code, and ethics policies;
- bibliography and citation rules;
- template, font, layout, file, and submission constraints;
- dates and time zones when the user asks for scheduling.

Compare requirements with the current repository and list compliant, missing, and uncertain items. Do not replace the official template with an approximate recreation. Recommend the strongest venue whose scope and evidence standards the work can defensibly meet; do not promise acceptance or inflate claims. Reverify requirements close to submission and whenever the venue year or track changes.
