# Citation verification

Audit each important citation-backed statement in the manuscript and `paper/claims.md`. Retrieve the cited primary source through Zotero, local full text, or an authoritative publisher/repository source. Treat search snippets and generated summaries as leads only.

For every consequential claim, record:

- manuscript location and exact proposition being supported;
- citation key and stable identifier;
- primary or secondary source status;
- evidence level: full text with locator, abstract only, or unverified;
- whether the source directly supports, partially supports, contradicts, or does not address the proposition;
- a precise revision or replacement when support is insufficient.

Verify title, authors, venue, year, DOI or preprint/OpenReview identifier, publication status, and citation key. Prefer Zotero/Better BibTeX metadata when available; never fabricate missing fields. Detect duplicate records and preprint/published-version duplicates, while preserving the version actually cited. Check the literature matrix for missing closest prior work.

Produce a durable verification report and correct only claims or metadata justified by evidence. Flag a claim that requires inaccessible full text rather than approving it from an abstract.
